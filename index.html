Where It All Began

Our story began in 2016 on the 7th floor of our college. We were in different groups of friends when one of Maria Ivy’s friends introduced Jose Marie to her. Maria Ivy happily started a conversation, while Jose Marie quietly listened—mostly because he couldn’t understand a word of Bisaya! Seeing an opportunity to help, Maria Ivy offered to teach him Bisaya… in exchange for Pancit Canton and C2. And apparently, that was a deal he couldn’t refuse.

After that day, Jose Marie started hanging out with Maria Ivy and her friends more often. What began as a simple introduction, a little language lesson, and a very important Pancit Canton and C2 arrangement slowly turned into a beautiful friendship—and eventually, a love story.

Neither of them knew it at the time, but that little introduction on the 7th floor was the beginning of their forever.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked

What time should we arrive?

Our ceremony begins at 1:30 PM at The Archdiocesan Shrine and Parish of Our Lady of Guadalupe de Cebu, on Labra Street, Barangay Guadalupe, Cebu City.

We’d suggest arriving a little ahead of time so everyone is comfortably seated before we begin.

What should we wear?

We would love to see you in your Semi Formal attire that suits in our colour motif — maroon or burgundy, dusty rose, plum, and navy blue.

We kindly ask our guests to avoid wearing striped patterns. This will help us keep the overall look of our celebration beautiful and cohesive. You’ll find our colour swatches, and a little attire inspiration, in the Dress Code section.

Our Principal and Secondary Sponsors have colours of their own — dusty pink for our ninangs and navy blue for our ninongs. The details are in the Dress Code section too.

Where will the reception be held?

Our reception follows at 4:00 PM at Jimie Hall, Waterfront Cebu City Hotel and Casino, Salinas Drive, Lahug, Cebu City. You’ll find directions in the Event Details section.

May I bring a guest?

Seats are reserved for the names on your invitation, and we are able to accommodate one guest per invitation.

We hope you understand — it simply helps us make sure there is a comfortable seat for everyone.

Are children welcome at the wedding?

We kindly request that our wedding be adults-only, except for children who have been specifically invited to be part of our ceremony or have a special role on our day.

Thank you so much for your understanding and consideration. We can’t wait to celebrate with you!

How and by when should we RSVP?

You can reply using the RSVP form on this page. Please let us know as soon as your plans are settled so we can finalise our arrangements.

If you have any trouble, please reach us using the contact details on your invitation.

Are you receiving gifts?

Your presence at our wedding is the greatest gift we could ever ask for.

If you would like to bless us with a gift, we would be truly grateful for a monetary gift, as we will be travelling back to Australia after our celebration and have limited space to bring physical gifts with us. Details are in the Gift Guide.
